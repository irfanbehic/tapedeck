Privacy policy

Applies to the Tapedeck iOS and iPadOS app. Last updated 4 September 2026.

The short version. Your recordings, their transcripts and their titles never leave your device. There is no account and we never learn who you are. The only thing sent off the device is an anonymous count of which screens are opened, and you can switch that off inside the app.

Who is responsible

Tapedeck is made and operated by İrfan Behiç Tırpancı, an independent developer based in Türkiye. For the purposes of the GDPR and similar laws, that is the data controller for the small amount of data described below. You can reach us at irfanbehic@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you make with Tapedeck is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage:

  • Audio recordings, including ones still in progress
  • Transcripts and every word in them
  • Recording titles, folders and markers
  • Anything you type into the search field
  • Your settings, including the theme and the analytics switch

None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else. Recording, playback and transcription make no network requests at all. Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech engine, which runs locally on your iPhone or iPad.

If you make an encrypted iPhone or iCloud backup, your recordings are included in that backup, the same as any other app's files. That copy is between you and Apple, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes every recording with it, and Settings inside the app has an Erase everything action that removes them without uninstalling.

What we do collect

Two things, both anonymous, neither of them linked to your identity, and neither used for tracking or advertising.

WhatWhyCan you turn it off
Anonymous product interaction. The name of a screen when it opens, and a handful of plain events such as a recording being started or the pricing screen being shown. Sent with a randomly generated identifier that is not connected to your Apple Account, your name, your email or your device's advertising identifier. To see which parts of the app people actually use, and which ones they get stuck on. Yes. Settings, then Privacy, then the analytics switch. Turning it off stops it immediately.

Apple also gives us the usual App Store reporting: aggregate download, sales and crash figures. That comes from Apple in summary form and never identifies an individual buyer. Apple's own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we never collect

  • Your audio, in whole or in part
  • Transcript text, recording titles, folder names or marker labels
  • Anything you type into search
  • Your name, email address, phone number or Apple Account
  • Contacts, photos, location, or your device's advertising identifier

There is no account to create, no login, and no password. We sell nothing to anyone, we run no advertising, and there is no third party advertising or attribution SDK in the app.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhat it is used for
MicrophoneRecording audio. That is the only thing it is used for, and recording only happens when you start it.
Speech recognitionTurning a recording into text on the device. Asked for only when you first transcribe something, and the audio is not uploaded.
NotificationsOptional. Telling you that a long recording is still running, that a transcript has finished, or that a recording was recovered after a crash.

Every one of these can be refused or withdrawn in the iOS Settings app. Refusing notifications costs you nothing but the reminders. Refusing the microphone means the app cannot record.

Who processes data for us

  • PostHog receives the anonymous product interaction events described above. Our project is hosted in PostHog's European Union region, so those events are stored in the EU. See PostHog's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat checks and caches your purchase state against Apple's receipt, under an anonymous identifier. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Apple processes the purchase itself, distributes the app, and gives us aggregate sales and crash reporting. We never see your payment details.

That is the complete list. No one else receives anything.

Legal basis and your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the analytics described above rest on our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used, kept narrow by making the data anonymous and by giving you a switch that turns it off. Purchase state rests on performing the contract you entered into when you bought Pro.

Because none of what we hold identifies you, we cannot look up "your" data, and there is nothing for us to hand over or delete on request. In practice your rights are exercised directly:

  • To stop collection: turn off the analytics switch in Settings, then Privacy.
  • To erase everything the app holds: use Erase everything in Settings, or delete the app.
  • To ask a question about any of this: write to irfanbehic@gmail.com and you will get an answer.

If you are in California, the same applies: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How long anything is kept

Your recordings and transcripts stay on your device until you delete them. Nothing expires them and nothing removes them but you. Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog for up to 12 months and then dropped. Purchase state is kept for as long as the purchase is valid, because that is what makes Restore purchase work.

Children

Tapedeck is not directed at children under 13, and it asks for no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will also be described in the app's release notes. We will not start collecting something new and quietly leave this page as it was.
