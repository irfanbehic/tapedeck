The promise

Four things, and the app is built around them.

Everything else is detail. These are the reasons to move.

The file is always playable

Audio is written to disk continuously, not assembled at the end. Kill the app mid-sentence and the recording up to that second opens and plays. There is no save button to forget.

It keeps going when the screen locks

Lock the phone, switch apps, take a call, let the battery run low. The recording continues. Nobody should have to turn off auto-lock to record a meeting.

Transcription runs on the device

Apple's own on-device speech engine turns a recording into text without uploading anything. Recording, playback and transcription make no network requests at all.

There is no account

No sign-up, no password, no email address. Your recordings live on your phone and belong to you. Buying is handled by your Apple Account, so we never learn who you are.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I lose my recordings if I do not pay?

No. Recording, autosave, recovery, playback and audio export never expire and are never limited. Pro only adds transcripts, search inside them, structured export and extra theme colours.

Is this a call recorder?

No. Tapedeck records through the microphone only. It does not record phone calls.

Where are my recordings stored?

On your device, inside the app's own storage. They are included in an encrypted iPhone or iCloud backup if you make one, and deleting the app deletes them with it.

Can I get my recordings out?

Yes. Any recording can be shared or exported as an audio file through the normal iOS share sheet, and with Pro a transcript can be exported as text, Markdown or SRT.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tapedeck. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us, so cancelling there is immediate and final.

I bought Pro and switched phones. What now?

Open Settings inside Tapedeck and tap Restore purchase. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it comes back without any login.
