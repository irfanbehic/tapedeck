Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations differ enormously between countries and, in the United States, between states. Some require the consent of everyone present. Complying with the law where you are is your responsibility, not ours, and Tapedeck gives you no permission or defence you would not otherwise have.

Tapedeck records through the device microphone only. It is not a call recorder and cannot record telephone calls.

Transcription accuracy

Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. It is good, and it is not perfect. Accents, crosstalk, background noise, technical vocabulary and poor microphone position all cost accuracy. Transcripts are provided as an aid and should not be relied on as a verbatim record for legal, medical, financial or any other consequential purpose without checking them against the audio.
